# What's new in EnlacesPanel (web and server)

<!-- Newest version on top. The Spanish list is in NOVEDADES.md. -->

## 0.5.0
- **Spanish or English:** choose the panel language in Account → Language (or on the sign-in
  screen). The first time it uses your browser language. The Android app follows the same language.
- **Language of each page** (Settings): the fixed texts of your public page (footer, privacy,
  legal notice) show in Spanish or English.
- **Footer with the versions** at the bottom of the panel: web, Android app and a link to what's new.

## 0.4.1
- **Pangolin guide** in Settings and in the README: how to publish EnlacesPanel with your domain
  from the VPS (HTTP resource, the Raspberry Pi IP and port 8092, without Pangolin authentication)
  and how to add a page's custom domain.
- **Uninstaller** `uninstall.sh`: one command, with a backup and questions before deleting anything.

## 0.4.0
- **Create users from the panel** (Users → Create user): name, password (or "Generate password")
  and whether they are an administrator. The account is active right away.
- **Change the password** of any user from the user list.
- **What's new on screen:** after an update, the panel shows once what changed. In Account → About
  you see the web and app versions and can read it again.

## 0.3.0
- **Photo or logo on each link** (or an emoji), on the left of the button.
- **Schedule dates with a switch:** when off, the link always shows and never expires.
- **Social icons** (WhatsApp, Telegram, email, phone, Instagram…), at the top or the bottom.
- **Animated page:** buttons come in one by one, shine on hover and press down when tapped.
  Soft, Lively or no animation. New themes: Aurora and Neon.
- **Phone / Tablet / Desktop** resizes the editor preview.

## 0.2.0
- The first time you sign in, your page is created and you go straight to the editor.
- **Settings:** address, published, home page, **custom domain** per page, public server address
  and open or closed sign-up.
- **View my page** button, full screen as phone, tablet or desktop.

## 0.1.0
- First version: link page on your server, editor with preview, themes, cookie-free stats,
  QR code, several pages and users, and Android app.
