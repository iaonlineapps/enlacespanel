# What's new in the EnlacesPanel Android app

## Version 1.1
- The app is in Spanish and English. It uses the language you choose in the panel (Account → Language) or, if you haven't chosen, the phone's language.

## Version 1.0.1
- In Account → About you see the web and app versions, and from there you can read what's new in the app or check for a new version.
- When the server is updated, the app shows once what changed in the web.

## Version 1.0
- First version: edit your link page from your phone, connected to your own EnlacesPanel server.
- Works with your domain (for example https://links.mydomain.com) and also with your home IP over wifi. If you enter both, the app uses whichever works.
- Upload your photo from the gallery, download your page's QR code and share it with any app.
- Tells you when there is a new version of the app.
